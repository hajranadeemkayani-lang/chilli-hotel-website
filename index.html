Craftsmanship & Heritage

Rooted in Ancient Spice Roads, Crafted for Modern Lovers

At Chilli & Spice, spice isn't just heat—it's a symphony of flavor, aroma, and complexity. Founded by Chef Vikram Raichand, our kitchen sources rare hand-harvested spices directly from single-estate farms across South Asia, Mexico, and the Caribbean.

Every curry is slow-simmered for over 8 hours, and every naan is slapped against live clay oven walls. We balance intense peppers with soothing cream, fragrant saffron, and fresh citrus.
